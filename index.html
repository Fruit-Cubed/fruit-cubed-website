Company Identity

Ugly fruit.
Beautifully reimagined.

Not every piece of fruit looks picture-perfect. Some are oddly shaped, slightly bruised or simply too small for supermarket shelves. But on the inside? They're still full of flavour and potential. We rescue this perfectly edible fruit and transform it into vibrant, convenient fruit powder.

Product Information

Small fruit. Big impact.

Better ingredients, better choices, better possibilities.

Food waste prevention

Many fruits in China and worldwide are discarded for cosmetic reasons only. This creates food waste, because these fruits are still perfectly safe for consumption. We save these fruits to process them into a powder.

Carbon emission reduction

Did you know that carbon emissions from food waste could have an even greater affect on the environment than coal plants worldwide? Our product, by reducing food waste, also helps lessen global warming.

Rehydrate for juice

You can then rehydrate our product with water to get a fruity drink that improves gut health. Our powder is directly made from fresh fruit, meaning that it contains the same vitamins.

From Fruit to Powder

How we make the magic happen.

Three simple steps. One delicious transformation.

01

Rescue

We source cosmetically imperfect but suitable-to-eat fruit that might otherwise be overlooked.

02

Dehydrate

The fruit is prepared and dehydrated to remove moisture while retaining its fruity character.

03

Transform

The dried fruit is milled into a fine, colourful powder ready to brighten up your everyday creations.

A Better Kind of Beauty

Pretty isn't everything.

We believe the value of fruit goes beyond its appearance. As explained in our product information, Fruit Cubed aims to make better use of existing food resources to develop a juice that is both more sustainable and healthy.
